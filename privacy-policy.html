Roostoo — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — Effective 4 September 2026

Roostoo Labs Limited, a company incorporated in Hong Kong ("Roostoo"), is the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this Policy.

Contact: contact@roostoo.com

1. Overview

This Policy sets out the personal data Roostoo collects in connection with the Roostoo website, applications and services (the "Platform"), the purposes for which that data is processed, the parties with whom it is shared, and the rights available to users in respect of it.

The scope of collection is limited by the architecture of the Platform. Roostoo does not take custody of user assets, does not hold user private keys or seed phrases, and operates on a self-custodial basis. The categories of personal data principally processed are account and authentication data, wallet addresses, Agent and Session records, and device and usage data.

Roostoo does not sell personal data, does not disclose personal data to data brokers, and does not process personal data for advertising purposes.

Certain activity conducted through the Platform is recorded on public blockchains. Such records are permanent and cannot be amended or deleted by Roostoo or by any other party. Section 8 sets out the consequences of this for deletion requests.

This overview is provided for convenience and does not qualify the provisions that follow.

Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Policy have the meanings given to them in the Roostoo Terms of Service.

2. Scope and Applicable Law

2.1 This Policy applies to all users of the Platform.

2.2 Roostoo is established in Hong Kong. Its processing of personal data is governed by the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) and the Data Protection Principles set out in that Ordinance.

2.3 The Platform is not offered to persons located in the United States, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, and Roostoo maintains access controls intended to prevent use from those jurisdictions. Roostoo has nonetheless aligned its practices with the standards established under those regimes. Where an individual is in fact entitled to rights under the General Data Protection Regulation, the UK General Data Protection Regulation or an applicable United States state privacy statute, Roostoo will give effect to those rights in accordance with Section 9.

3.4 Data not collected

Roostoo does not collect, request or hold user private keys, seed phrases, or any credential capable of effecting a transfer of user assets. Roostoo will not request such information under any circumstances, and users should not disclose it to any person purporting to act on Roostoo's behalf.

3.5 Treatment of wallet addresses

A wallet address does not of itself disclose a user's identity, but may be associated with an identifiable individual, particularly when combined with on-chain transaction history or with other data held by Roostoo. Roostoo treats wallet addresses, and activity associated with them, as personal data.

5. Automated Processing

5.1 Trading decisions. Where a user deploys an Agent, the Agent determines which instruments to trade, in which direction, in what size and at what time, within the limits established by the user in the Agent Mandate. Such determinations concern market conditions rather than the individual user, and are not subject to individual human review. The operation of Agents is described in Parts C and E of the Terms of Service.

5.2 Determinations concerning individuals. Roostoo applies automated controls for the purposes of eligibility assessment, geolocation and fraud prevention. Such controls may result in access being refused, a Session being halted, or an account being suspended. A user in respect of whom an automated determination is made adversely may request human review by written request to contact@roostoo.com, save where a legal or regulatory obligation prevents Roostoo from disclosing the matter.

5.3 Competition integrity. Automated anomaly detection may identify an entry for review. A determination to disqualify an entry or to recover a prize under clause 9.4 of the Terms of Service is subject to human review before it is made.

6. Disclosure of Personal Data

6.1 Roostoo discloses personal data only as set out in this Section. Roostoo does not sell personal data, does not share personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising, and does not disclose personal data to data brokers.

  • Venues. Where an Agent transacts, order and account data is exchanged with the Venue selected by the user. Venues are independent controllers in respect of the data they hold and process it under their own privacy policies. Roostoo does not control that processing.
  • Wallet and infrastructure providers, including Privy, together with cloud hosting, database, RPC and market data providers, each acting on Roostoo's instructions.
  • Analytics and infrastructure providers, including Dune Analytics, each engaged for the purposes described in Section 4.
  • Professional advisers, including legal, audit and accounting advisers, in each case subject to duties of confidence.
  • Governmental authorities, courts and regulators, where required by law or regulation, or where necessary for the establishment or defence of legal claims.
  • A successor entity, in connection with a sale, merger or reorganisation of the business or any part of it.

6.2 Publicly visible information. Agent names, Statistics, leaderboard positions and Competition results are visible to other users of the Platform. Roostoo does not publish a user's legal name without that user's consent. Where a user selects a username or Agent name that identifies them, that information becomes public as a consequence of the user's own selection.

7. International Transfers

7.1 Roostoo is established in Hong Kong and engages service providers operating in a number of jurisdictions. Personal data may accordingly be processed outside the jurisdiction in which a user is located.

7.2 Roostoo selects service providers having regard to their security practices and to the terms on which they process data, and discloses to each provider only the personal data required for the purpose for which it is engaged. Service providers process personal data in accordance with their own published terms and privacy policies.

7.3 Roostoo is progressively putting written data processing agreements in place with its service providers. Where a transfer of personal data requires a specific transfer mechanism under an applicable law, Roostoo will implement that mechanism before the transfer is made.

8. Blockchain Data and Limitations on Deletion

8.1 Certain activity conducted through the Platform is recorded on public blockchains, including Competition entries, prize settlements and transactions associated with a user's wallet address.

8.2 Records written to a public blockchain are public, permanent and outside the control of any party, including Roostoo. Such records cannot be amended, concealed or deleted. A request for deletion of personal data cannot extend to them.

8.3 This limitation arises from the nature of the underlying technology rather than from any operational decision of Roostoo. Users should understand, before using the Platform, that a wallet address and its associated on-chain activity are permanently public and may be inspected by any person.

9. Rights of Individuals

9.1 Under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, an individual may request confirmation as to whether Roostoo holds personal data concerning them and a copy of such data, request correction of personal data that is inaccurate, and request information as to Roostoo's policies and practices in relation to personal data.

9.2 Where an individual is entitled to rights under another applicable privacy law, including the General Data Protection Regulation, the UK General Data Protection Regulation or a United States state privacy statute, Roostoo will give effect to those rights. Depending upon the law in question, such rights may include rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability, the right to withdraw consent, and the right not to be subjected to discriminatory treatment for having exercised a right.

9.3 Exercise of rights. Requests should be submitted to contact@roostoo.com. Roostoo will respond within the period prescribed by the applicable law and in any event without undue delay. Roostoo may take steps to verify that a request originates from the individual concerned, ordinarily by requesting a signed message from the wallet associated with the relevant account.

9.4 Limitations. Roostoo may be unable to delete or cease processing personal data where it is required for compliance with a legal or regulatory record-keeping obligation, for the settlement of an open Session, for the establishment or defence of legal claims, or for the maintenance of the integrity records described at Section 3.2. Section 8 applies to any data recorded on a public blockchain. Where a request is refused in whole or in part, Roostoo will notify the individual of the reasons for the refusal.

9.5 Complaints. An individual dissatisfied with Roostoo's handling of personal data is invited to raise the matter with Roostoo at contact@roostoo.com in the first instance. An individual may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data, Hong Kong, or with the supervisory authority in their own jurisdiction where one exists.

10. Retention

Category Retention period

Session, order, Agent-decision and Competition records 7 years, consistent with clause 28.5 of the Terms of Service
Verification records 7 years following termination of the relationship, or such longer period as applicable legislation requires
Support correspondence and complaints 7 years
Analytics and log data 24 months
Marketing consents and withdrawals Until withdrawn, and a record of the withdrawal thereafter

Roostoo may retain personal data for a longer period where required by law, by a regulator or by a court, or where necessary in connection with an unresolved dispute. Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, personal data is deleted or anonymised such that it can no longer be attributed to an individual.

11. Security

11.1 Roostoo maintains technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risks presented by its processing, including encryption in transit, access controls and least-privilege access to production systems, segregation of trading credentials from user-identifying data, logging and monitoring, and periodic review of service providers.

11.2 Trading API Keys held by Roostoo on a user's behalf carry trade-only permissions, do not permit withdrawal or transfer of assets, are stored in encrypted form, and are revocable by the user at any time.

11.3 No system of security is absolute. Where a breach affecting personal data occurs and applicable law requires notification to affected individuals or to a supervisory authority, Roostoo will provide such notification without undue delay.

11.4 User-side security. Roostoo does not hold user private keys or seed phrases. The security of a user's wallet accordingly rests with the user. Roostoo will not at any time request a private key or seed phrase, and any communication purporting to do so on Roostoo's behalf should be treated as fraudulent.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

12.1 Roostoo uses cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary for the operation of the Platform, including for the maintenance of authenticated sessions, the retention of user preferences, and the prevention of fraud and abuse.

12.2 Roostoo additionally uses analytics cookies and similar technologies to understand how the Platform is used and to inform its improvement. Users may restrict or delete cookies through their browser settings, although doing so may affect the operation of the Platform.

12.3 Roostoo does not use advertising cookies and does not engage in cross-site tracking.

13. Minors

The Platform is intended for persons aged 18 years or over. Roostoo does not knowingly collect personal data from persons under that age. Where Roostoo becomes aware that it has done so, the data will be deleted and the relevant account closed. Any person who believes that a minor is using the Platform is asked to notify Roostoo at contact@roostoo.com.

14. Amendments

Roostoo may amend this Policy from time to time. An amended Policy will be published bearing a version number and effective date. Where an amendment materially affects the manner in which personal data is processed, Roostoo will provide notice by email or through the Platform before the amendment takes effect.

15. Contact

Enquiries, requests and complaints in relation to this Policy should be addressed to contact@roostoo.com.
